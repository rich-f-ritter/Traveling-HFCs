# Large Housing Authorities — Houston HA & Cameron County HA

Phase 1 sponsor profiles for two Chapter 392 housing authorities in the seed. Both are **Tier 3
(Ch. 392)** — a *separate* legal regime from the HFC/PFC target set. The controlling legal fact,
confirmed below, is that **HB 21 (2025) and HB 2071 (2023) do NOT reach a housing authority's own
Chapter 392 exemption** — so these deals do **not** die on the 1/1/2027 cliff. Their exposure is
(a) the area-of-operation *ultra vires* theory, (b) the §392.005(c) affordability condition added in
2023, and (c) political/journalistic pressure — not a statutory kill switch.

Analysts are explicit that, post-HB 21, "the PHA exemption is the least regulated TEPPs tool" and
expect **new** traveling activity to migrate *to* Chapter 392 (Holland & Knight / Butler Snow). The
census should treat these HAs as a *growing*, not dying, structure.

---

## The Chapter 392 legal frame (applies to both)

- **Species / statute:** Housing authority, Local Gov't Code **Ch. 392** ("Housing Authorities
  Established by Municipalities and Counties"). Distinct from Ch. 394 HFCs and Ch. 303 PFCs.
- **Tax exemption — §392.005:** "The property of an authority is public property used for essential
  public and governmental purposes," and the authority and its property are "exempt from all taxes
  and special assessments." **There is NO geographic clause tying the exemption to the area of
  operation** — unlike the post-reform HFC (§394.031(c), HB 21) and PFC (§303.021(d), HB 2071)
  regimes. This is precisely why HA deals survive the 2027 cliff and why the area-of-operation
  attack on them is a *common-law/ultra vires* argument rather than a statutory one.
- **2023 amendment (HB 2071, eff. 6/18/2023) added §392.005(c) / (c-1):** an HA multifamily
  development owned by a **"housing development corporation or similar entity created by a housing
  authority, other than a public facility corporation under Ch. 303"** is exempt only if (1) the HA
  holds a **public hearing** to approve it AND (2) **≥50% of units are reserved for households <80%
  AMI**. Developments owned through an HA-sponsored **Ch. 303 PFC** fall under §392.005(c-1) + the
  Ch. 303 audit regime (HB 2071), not this subsection. *Open question:* whether pre-6/18/2023 deals
  are grandfathered from §392.005(c) — not confirmed in public sources; flag per deal.
- **Area of operation:**
  - **Municipal HA (§392.014):** "the municipality for which the authority is created and the area
    that is within five miles of the territorial boundaries of the municipality and is **not** within
    the territorial boundaries of another municipality."
  - **County HA (§392.015):** "the county in which the authority is created **excluding** the parts of
    the county that are within the territorial boundaries of a municipality."
- **HB 21 (2025):** amends Ch. 394 (HFCs) only. Legal analysts confirm it **"does not apply to public
  housing authorities governed by Chapter 392."** No 2027 cliff, no TDHCA §394.9027 audit for direct
  HA deals. (An HA-sponsored *PFC* deal WOULD sit in the Ch. 303 / HB 2071 / TDHCA-audit lane.)

## 1. Houston Housing Authority (HHA)

**(1) Species & affiliated instrumentality.** Housing authority, **Ch. 392** (municipal; formerly
"Housing Authority of the City of Houston"). HHA runs BOTH tracks: its **own Ch. 392 deals** *and* a
**Chapter 303 public facility corporation** (the PFC program HHA has used since 2015). New CEO **Jamie
Bryant** (took office Feb 2025) publicly **halted all new PFC *and* Chapter 392 projects** — a
voluntary stop under political/press pressure, not a statutory sunset. In the 19 seed rows, HHA
appears as the **direct fee owner / "Ground Leasor"** on the ground-lease deals ("99-year unsubordinated
net ground lease held by Houston Housing Authority"), i.e. the **§392.005 direct-authority-property**
exemption theory — NOT (on the seed evidence) routed through the PFC. The affiliated Ch. 303 PFC
exists and is used for other HHA deals; its exact registered name should be pulled from the TDHCA PFC
monitoring list / Comptroller before attributing any specific seed row to it.

**(2) Area of operation.** City of Houston + 5 miles beyond the city limits, excluding any other
municipality (§392.014). Houston's limits are large and irregular, so most inner-loop and near-in
unincorporated Harris County communities fall inside; distant exurbs and parcels inside *other* cities
do not.

**(3) Governance / administrator.** Public housing authority governed by a mayor-appointed Board of
Commissioners; CEO **Jamie Bryant** (2025–). Large, legitimate PHA — conventional public housing +
HCV/Section 8 + tax-credit portfolio, of which the fee-flip/ground-lease deals are a subset.

**(4) Geographic reach — LOCAL vs TRAVELING (19 seed rows).**

| County | Rows | Seed flag | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Harris** | 15 | local | Presumptively **LOCAL** — Houston + 5 mi covers most. Caveat: parcels sitting *inside another municipality* (e.g. City of Katy, City of Friendswood) or >5 mi from Houston limits (far Spring/Cypress/Katy exurbs) are technically out-of-area. 2–4 are borderline and need parcel-geocode-vs-city-limit checks. |
| **Fort Bend** (Richmond ×2) | 2 | traveling | **TRAVELING** — Richmond ~30 mi SW, own county, well outside 5-mi ring. |
| **Montgomery** (Kingwood, Spring) | 2 | traveling | **TRAVELING** — Montgomery County, north of Houston, outside area of operation. |

Seed flags **4 of 19 traveling**; that count is geographically defensible as a floor. With
municipal-boundary refinements it could reach **~6** (if some "Harris" parcels sit inside Katy/
Friendswood city limits). Bottom line: **HHA is mostly LOCAL.**

**(5) Kill regime.** For the ~13–15 local Harris deals: **no kill — stable** (core PHA exemption). For
the ~4–6 out-of-area deals: **still no statutory kill** — HB 21 does not reach Ch. 392 and §392.005
has no geographic self-destruct. Real exposure = (i) *ultra vires* / area-of-operation challenge
(a court holding out-of-area acquisition void would trigger back-assessment up to 5 years under Tax
Code §11.43(i)); (ii) §392.005(c) affordability compliance — seed rows marked `affordable: None`
(Richmond 1274045, Kingwood 4408, Katy 5940, Humble 4391, Cypress 2405) are the most exposed if not
grandfathered/otherwise structured; (iii) HHA's own voluntary halt. **Faces 2027 cliff: NO** (unless a
given row is actually held by HHA's Ch. 303 PFC, which would put it in the HB 2071 grandfather/audit
lane — verify per row).

**(6) Portfolio scale.** Large. Houston Chronicle investigation counted ~100+ Houston tax-exempt
apartment deals across the PFC + Ch. 392 programs; **HHA has collected ~$53M in fees**; "tens of
thousands" of tax-exempt units, of which only ~1% serve very-low-income renters. One complex saved
$1.38M in taxes vs. $405K forgone rent. The 19 seed rows are a subset of a much larger HHA structured
portfolio.

**(7) Litigation / AG / news.** **No tax-exemption-structure litigation against HHA found.** Exposure
is journalistic/political: sustained Houston Chronicle / Texas Standard / Bisnow coverage of the
fee-vs-affordability mismatch, which drove the CEO's voluntary halt and 2025 reform bills (HHA then
shifted between PFC and Ch. 392 to stay ahead of reform scope). Separate, unrelated litigation exists
over environmental contamination at an HHA development (Independence Heights II / Pointe at Bayou Bend)
— not a tax matter.

**(8) Open questions.** (a) Exact registered name of HHA's Ch. 303 PFC and which (if any) seed rows run
through it vs. direct Ch. 392. (b) Parcel-level geocode of the 15 Harris rows vs. Houston city-limit +
5-mi polygon, and whether any sit inside another municipality → refine the traveling count. (c)
Grandfather status of pre-6/18/2023 deals under §392.005(c). (d) Whether any out-of-area host
(Richmond/Fort Bend, Montgomery) has challenged the exemption. **Likely traveling: ~4 of 19 (floor),
up to ~6; the other ~13–15 are LOCAL.**

## 2. Cameron County Housing Authority (Cameron County HA)

**⚠ Entity-identity is the central issue.** There are TWO Cameron County public corporations that must
not be conflated:

- **Cameron County Housing AUTHORITY** — Ch. 392, **Brownsville** (65 Castellano Cir), Exec. Director
  **Daisy Flores**, Board chaired by Dr. Norma Linda Gallegos. Stated mission = affordable housing
  *within Cameron County*. This is the seed's named sponsor.
- **Cameron County Housing FINANCE Corporation (CCHFC)** — Ch. 394 **HFC**, **San Benito** (216 S. Sam
  Houston Blvd), profiled elsewhere in this project (30 seed rows). This is the entity in the CBS I-Team
  investigation: it bought Oak Park Apartments in **Euless (~536 mi away)** and owns property in Dallas,
  Fort Worth, McKinney, Irving, Lewisville + a dozen cities; collects ~15% of tax savings. **Williamson
  County has sued CCHFC** for lack of authority to acquire/exempt property outside Cameron County; TWHC
  intervened. CCHFC is a Tier-1, HB-21-cliff entity.

The seed attributes 4 Houston/Galveston deals to the **Authority**, and one ground-lease comment
explicitly names *"Cameron County Housing Authority"* as holder of an 85-year net ground lease
(§392.005 direct-ownership pattern). Note the seed's metadata lists the HA's home city as "San Benito"
— which is actually the **HFC's** address, evidence the seed partly conflated the two. **This
attribution needs deed-level confirmation** (grantee name + statute chapter on the recorded SWD /
memorandum of ground lease). Working assumption: these ARE genuine HA deals (the HA's Houston/Galveston
footprint is geographically distinct from the HFC's documented North Texas footprint, which supports
them being separate), but confidence is **medium** pending record confirmation.

**(1) Species & affiliated instrumentality.** As-seeded: Housing authority, **Ch. 392** (county). No
separate HA-sponsored PFC identified; the *sister* HFC (CCHFC, Ch. 394) is the well-documented
traveling vehicle. If any of the 4 rows is in fact a CCHFC deal, it re-tiers to Tier 1 (HB 21 cliff).

**(2) Area of operation (§392.015).** Cameron County, excluding incorporated municipalities within it.
Cameron County is at the far southern tip of Texas (~300+ mi from Houston/Galveston).

**(3) Governance / administrator.** Board of Commissioners (Chair Dr. Norma Linda Gallegos; Vice-Chair
Ronaldo Garcia; members Maru De La Paz, Juan Andrade Jr.; Resident Commissioner Rosario Torres);
Executive Director **Daisy Flores**. Small county PHA.

**(4) Geographic reach — LOCAL vs TRAVELING (4 seed rows).** All four are hundreds of miles outside
Cameron County → **100% TRAVELING** (if held by the HA):

| census_id | Property city | County | Partner |
|---|---|---|---|
| TX-63-3735 | Houston | Harris | Alliance Development |
| TX-63-403 | Stafford | Harris | Leste |
| TX-62-3053 | League City | Galveston | Post Investment Group |
| TX-62-4070 | League City | Galveston | Post Investment Group |

(Post Investment Group ties to "Post WB Apartments LLC," the HB 21 constitutional-challenge plaintiff —
a traveling-deal developer network.)

**(5) Kill regime.** As Ch. 392 HA deals: **no statutory 2027 cliff** (HB 21/2071 don't reach Ch. 392).
But these are the *strongest ultra vires cases in either authority's book* — a county HA operating 300+
mi outside its county has essentially no area-of-operation nexus. The Williamson County v. CCHFC suit,
RQ-0566-KP, and the general "traveling" litigation wave all press the same out-of-area theory; an
adverse ruling (even against the HFC) is precedent that would threaten these HA deals, with 5-year
back-assessment (Tax Code §11.43(i)). §392.005(c) affordability also applies (row 403 Stafford =
`affordable: None`; row 4070 = `Partial`). **Faces 2027 cliff: NO as HA; YES if any row is actually a
CCHFC/Ch. 394 deal — verify.**

**(6) Portfolio scale.** Seed = 4 rows (small). The *HFC* sibling is the large traveling portfolio
(30 seed rows + dozens more statewide).

**(7) Litigation / AG / news.** No suit found against the **Authority** specifically. Heavy exposure
attaches to the **HFC** sibling (CBS I-Team series; Williamson County suit; Euless/North Texas backlash;
named in the HB 21 reform narrative). The HB 21 constitutional challenge (TWHC + Post WB Apartments v.
Bexar Appraisal District) was filed in **Cameron County District Court** (venue), Sept 2025.

**(8) Open questions.** (a) **Deed-level confirmation** that each of the 4 rows is held by the
**Authority** (Ch. 392) vs the **HFC** (Ch. 394) — the single most important verification. (b) If HA:
were public hearings held and do the deals meet §392.005(c) (≥50% <80% AMI)? (c) Any host-jurisdiction
(Houston, Stafford, League City) challenge to the exemptions. **Likely traveling: 4 of 4 (all
out-of-area); species/tier of each pending record check.**
