## 1. Legal species — CONFIRMED HFC, Local Gov't Code Ch. 394

Garland Housing Finance Corporation ("GHFC") is a **Housing Finance Corporation under the Texas
Housing Finance Corporations Act, Local Government Code Chapter 394** — not a PFC (Ch. 303) and
not the housing authority. Cross-validated:

- **Court decision (primary).** *Collin Central Appraisal District v. Garland Housing Finance
  Corporation and TX Collin Apartments, L.P.*, No. 05-19-01417-CV (Tex. App.—Dallas [5th Dist.]
  Feb. 23, 2021): the court states GHFC "is a housing finance corporation (HFC) formed under the
  Texas Housing Finance Corporations Act (THFCA)" and analyzes the exemption under **§394.905**.
- **TDHCA "Local Housing Finance Corporation (HFC) Contact List"** includes Garland HFC.
- GHFC's own site (garlandhfc.org) describes HFC-type functions: bond issuance, direct ownership,
  down-payment assistance, multifamily/senior development.

**Exemption theory:** §394.905 — HFC property is "exempt, as public property used for public
purposes, from … all other taxes" → 100% ad valorem exemption (the same exemption HB 21 now
conditions/kills for out-of-jurisdiction deals).

### Distinguished from the Garland Housing Authority (do not conflate)

There are **two separate Garland public housing bodies**:

| | Garland Housing Finance Corporation (THIS profile) | Garland Housing Agency / Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Statute | **Ch. 394 (HFC)** | Ch. 392 (housing authority) |
| Function | Fee ownership / ground-lease of multifamily; bonds; DPA | Administers HUD Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) program |
| Funding | Fees from deals, bonds | 100% HUD-funded |
| Head | Executive Director **David Gibbons**; Administrator **Marta Madera** | Executive Director **Steve Fitch** (SFitch@GarlandTX.gov) |
| Web | garlandhfc.org | garlandtx.gov/439/Garland-Housing-Agency |

The traveling multifamily deals in the seed belong to the **HFC (Ch. 394)**. The Housing Agency
(voucher program) is not the sponsor of any seed property.

- **Office:** 1675 W Campbell Rd, Garland, TX 75044 (PO Box 461243, Garland, TX 75046); (469) 716-4629.

**Critical for HB 21:** the HFC's *authorized area* under HB 21's new §394.031(c) is the
**sponsor's boundaries = the City of Garland municipal limits**, NOT all of Dallas County. This
matters in §4.

## 5. Kill regime — HB 21 §13(i) → 1/1/2027 (subject to §13(e) early death)

All four are HFC (Ch. 394) out-of-jurisdiction developments owned by GHFC (owner or ground-leasor
of the fee), so:

- **§13(i) — 1/1/2027 cliff.** A development owned by an HFC on **9/1/2025** and located outside
  its authorized area is **ineligible for the §394.905 exemption after 1/1/2027** unless the host
  §394.031(d) resolutions are obtained before then. Hosts that must resolve: **City of Plano,
  City of Allen, City of Fort Worth, City of Carrollton** (all incorporated; no county
  resolutions needed since none is unincorporated).
- **§13(e) — early death.** Exemption also ends after the first tax year following a
  **refinancing, title conveyance, or majority beneficial-interest transfer** (or the 10th tax
  year). Any of these four that trades/refis converts to full tax earlier than 2027.
- **§394.9027 audit forfeiture.** Failure to file the annual TDHCA audit (first due 6/1/2026)
  forfeits the exemption for that tax year — a possible earlier, independent death.

**Estimated kill date:** **1/1/2027** for all four (first full-tax bill ~Oct 2027, cash due
Jan 2028) absent host resolutions — earlier if a §13(e) capital event or an audit non-filing
intervenes.

**Host resolutions:** **NONE found** for Plano/Allen/Fort Worth/Carrollton as of this pass
(open question — needs each city's council-agenda check). Absent them, all four are captured.

**Irony worth noting for underwriting:** GHFC *litigated and won* the out-of-jurisdiction
exemption for the Plano property pre-HB 21 (see §7). HB 21 §13(i) legislatively overrides exactly
that result going forward — the court win does not survive the statute.

## 7. Litigation / AG / news exposure

- **Collin Central Appraisal District v. Garland Housing Finance Corporation and TX Collin
  Apartments, L.P.**, No. **05-19-01417-CV**, Tex. App.—Dallas (5th Dist.), **Feb. 23, 2021**
  (appeal from 471st Dist. Ct., Collin Co.). CCAD denied the §394.905 exemption on the
  **Savannah at Gateway Senior Living** complex in **Plano**, arguing City of Plano approval was
  required under §394.005. **Court AFFIRMED for GHFC** — no evidence Plano's approval was required;
  GHFC and its property exempt under Ch. 394. GHFC = fee owner of land; TX Collin Apartments, L.P.
  = ground lessee owning the improvements (classic HFC ground-lease structure). *Pre-HB 21 — a
  binding precedent for the traveling model that HB 21 now overrides prospectively.*
- **News:** The Real Deal (Oct 2024) flags Garland HFC by name as a top-2 statewide traveler in
  the loophole coverage that preceded HB 21 (Bettencourt reform push).
- **Governance:** City of Garland internal audits of GHFC (City Auditor Craig Hametner) — oversight,
  not adverse litigation.
- **Not found:** any naming of Garland in AG RQ-0566-KP or in the HB 21 constitutional-challenge
  roster (Cameron County / TX Workforce Housing Coalition). Absence unconfirmed — flag.
