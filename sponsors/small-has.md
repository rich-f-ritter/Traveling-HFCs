# Phase 1 — Small Housing Authorities (Ch. 392): Rosenberg · San Benito · Plano

Three small municipal housing authorities from the Tier-3 (HA) slice of the seed. The common
thread the seed missed: **each holds its tax-exempt apartment deals through an affiliated
Public Facility Corporation (Ch. 303), not through the HA's own §392.005 exemption** — so the
governing kill-regime is **HB 2071 (PFC)**, and the geographic test is the HA sponsor's
**"area of operation."** For a *municipal* HA that area is, by statute (§392.014), only the
**city + 5 miles** (excluding other cities) — **not the whole county.** That distinction is
decisive here and overturns the seed's same-county "local" coding for Rosenberg.

**Legislative note (kill-regime context):** **SB 1454 (89R, 2025)** would have cut the
municipal-HA area of operation to *inside city limits only* and required host-subdivision
resolutions for out-of-city projects. It **passed the Senate (5/9/2025) but died in House
Intergovernmental Affairs (last action 5/12/2025) — it is NOT law.** The §392.014 city+5mi
rule therefore still stands; the 2025 attempt to close the municipal-HA traveling loophole
failed. HB 21 (Ch. 394 HFC / 2027 cliff) does **not** apply to any of these — none is an HFC.

## 1. Rosenberg Housing Authority (Fort Bend County) — 5 properties

- **Species / statute:** Housing Authority of the City of Rosenberg — **Ch. 392** (municipal HA).
- **Affiliated instrumentality (holds the deals):** **Rosenberg Housing Authority Public
  Facility Corporation (RHAPFC)**, created **May 2021** under Ch. 303 (§303.021). Fee-title /
  ground-lessor vehicle for the tax-exempt apartment deals; exemption theory is **§303.042
  (PFC)**, so **HB 2071 governs**, not the HA's §392.005 exemption. Charter states a
  *Fort Bend County* scope and levies a $5,000 partnership-application fee.
- **Home jurisdiction / area of operation:** City of Rosenberg (Fort Bend Co.). Statutory
  area of operation = **Rosenberg city + 5 miles** (§392.014). A PFC sponsored by an HA may
  own multifamily only within that area of operation (§303.021(d), post-HB 2071).
- **Governance/admin:** Kimberly Brown administers the PFC (rhapfc@rosenbergha.org; 117 Lane
  Dr, Ste 22, Rosenberg). Board of the HA meets with published minutes (e.g., 11/13/2023
  minutes document the Retreat at Riverstone acquisition/rehab).
- **The 5 seed properties (all "Fort Bend County," which the seed read as LOCAL — incorrectly):**
  | census_id | City | Role | Deal note |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | TX-63-3263 | Sugar Land | Additional owner | recap 04/2025 (post-HB 2071); Catalyst Equity |
  | TX-63-334 | Stafford | Additional owner | 100% affordable; Madera Companies |
  | TX-63-4944 | Sugar Land | Additional owner | partial affordable (likely the "essential housing" Retreat at Riverstone) |
  | TX-63-8200 | Richmond | Primary Owner | Waterview Apts (295u), acquired **02/2026** (post-HB 2071); Velocity Capital |
  | TX-63-939655 | Richmond | Primary Owner | Haven at Bellaire (297u), acquired **09/2025** (post-HB 2071) |
- **LOCAL vs TRAVELING — the key finding (corrects seed):** The seed flags all five
  `traveling = no` on a **same-county** heuristic. But a *municipal* HA's area of operation is
  **city + 5 mi**, not the county. **Sugar Land (~12–15 mi) and Stafford (~18 mi) are outside
  Rosenberg's area of operation → these are TRAVELING** even though inside Fort Bend County.
  Confirmed anchor: **Retreat at Riverstone, 18545 University Blvd, Sugar Land 77479** (Riverstone
  master-planned community) is RHAPFC "essential housing" — an unambiguous out-of-area hold.
  The two **Richmond** deals (Waterview, Haven at Bellaire) *may* be local (Richmond adjoins
  Rosenberg) but "Richmond" mailing addresses sprawl east through unincorporated Fort Bend;
  need parcel addresses to confirm within-5-mi. → at least 3 of 5 travel.
- **Kill-regime:** **HB 2071 (Ch. 303), no 2027 cliff.** Two exposures: (a) pre-HB 2071 deals
  (Retreat at Riverstone, The Trestles, ~2021–22) are **grandfathered but out-of-area** →
  exposed to the statewide PFC constitutional/AG scrutiny, not auto-killed; (b) the **2025–26
  acquisitions** (Sugar Land recap 04/25; Haven at Bellaire 09/25; Waterview 02/26) are
  **post-HB 2071** — where out-of-area, **§303.021(d) bars PFC ownership → exemption arguably
  invalid from inception** (back-assessable), an earlier and different death than any cliff.
- **Litigation/news:** No Rosenberg-specific suit found. Fort Bend PFC deals (incl. Retreat at
  Riverstone — 2024 FBCAD value $57.7M, ~$1.25M/yr foregone taxes) criticized in local press
  (Katy Christian Magazine, 10/2024). Statewide PFC/AG scrutiny applies.
- **Scope verdict:** **WATCH** — re-classify the Sugar Land/Stafford holds as traveling; resolve
  the Richmond pair by address. Confidence **med**.

## 2. San Benito Housing Authority (Cameron County) — 1 property

- **Species / statute:** Housing Authority of the City of San Benito — **Ch. 392** (municipal HA).
  **Distinct from** Cameron County HA (county HA, 4 props) and **The Cameron County HFC**
  (Tier-1 Ch. 394 HFC, 30 props, also San-Benito-addressed) — do not conflate.
- **Affiliated instrumentality:** **None found.** No "San Benito PFC/HFC" located in public
  sources; the seed attributes the deal to the HA directly. Vehicle unconfirmed.
- **Home jurisdiction / area of operation:** City of San Benito (Cameron Co.). Statutory area
  of operation = San Benito city + 5 mi (§392.014). (Voucher/Section-8 service reaches Cameron
  County, but that is not the Ch. 392 area of operation.)
- **Governance/admin:** HA at 1400 N Reagan St, San Benito; brands as "Better Community Housing"
  (sanbenitohousing.com); ~209 public-housing units + Section-8. Board/ED names not surfaced.
- **Seed property:** **TX-53-1154589 — "Prospective," Primary Owner, McAllen (Hidalgo Co.),**
  100% affordable. **McAllen is ~40 mi west, in a different county (Hidalgo)** — entirely outside
  San Benito's area of operation.
- **LOCAL vs TRAVELING:** **TRAVELING** (clearly out-of-area) **— IF it exists.** Status is
  "Prospective"; no recorded deal, TDHCA filing, or Hidalgo CAD exemption confirmed. May be an
  announced/planned deal that has not closed.
- **Kill-regime:** No 2027 cliff (not an HFC). If it closes **via a PFC → HB 2071 §303.021(d)
  bars out-of-area ownership → ineligible/non-compliant from inception**; if **direct HA (Ch. 392)**,
  operating in McAllen would require McAllen + Hidalgo County cooperation-agreement resolutions
  (none evidenced). Either way the structure is legally dubious.
- **Litigation/news:** None found.
- **Scope verdict:** **WATCH** — genuine traveling profile but the deal is unconfirmed/prospective.
  Confidence **low**.

## 3. Plano Housing Authority (Collin County) — 1 property

- **Species / statute:** Housing Authority of the City of Plano — **Ch. 392** (municipal HA;
  Plano straddles Collin + a sliver of Denton).
- **Affiliated instrumentality (holds the deal):** **Plano Public Facility Corporation
  (Plano PFC)**, created by Plano HA (501(c)(3)); self-describes as operating "across multiple
  Texas counties." Board: **James Tucker (chair), Erica Dotras (vice-chair), Michael Kist**;
  offices 1740 G Avenue, Plano. Exemption theory **§303.042 (PFC) → HB 2071 governs**. Plano PFC
  has completed **14 developments since 2020 — 13 in Plano (local) + 1 in Lewisville (traveling).**
- **Home jurisdiction / area of operation:** City of Plano (Collin Co.); area of operation =
  Plano city + 5 mi (§392.014).
- **Seed property:** **TX-43-2392 — Ground-lessor role, Lewisville (Denton Co.) =
  Oak Tree Village Apartments**, 272 units, built 1985 (post-15-yr LIHTC), owner/developer
  **AMCAL Equities**; Plano PFC holds an **unsubordinated 99-yr ground lease expiring 09/30/2120**
  (→ commenced ~Sept 2021), 100% property-tax exemption; refi'd via a Greystone $43.2M HUD loan.
- **LOCAL vs TRAVELING:** **TRAVELING** — Lewisville (Denton Co.) is ~20 mi SW of Plano, far
  outside the city+5mi area of operation. This is Plano PFC's single out-of-jurisdiction deal.
- **Kill-regime:** **HB 2071 (Ch. 303), no 2027 cliff.** Ground lease ~Sept 2021 predates HB 2071
  (2023) → **grandfathered** (not auto-killed), but **audit-vulnerable** (TDHCA §303.0426; PFC
  audits due 6/1 annually) and, more pointedly, **under active local litigation** (below) —
  which, not a statutory cliff, is the realistic early-unwind vector.
- **Litigation/news:** **City of Plano + Plano ISD v. Plano Housing Authority & its PFCs**,
  Collin County District Court (219th), **Cause No. 219-03003-2024** — petition (initial filing
  reported 10/17/2023; escalated to a constitutional PFC challenge reported ~May 2025) demanding
  compliance/affordability records for the 14 PFC developments (4,143 units; 2,286 "affordable")
  and arguing the PFC ad valorem exemptions violate the Texas Constitution; subpoenas/depositions
  sought. Covered by NBC5 DFW, CBS Texas, Dallas Express. Oak Tree Village is one of the 14.
- **Scope verdict:** **IN** (confirmed traveling PFC deal; Tier-2 / HB 2071 grandfathered, **not**
  a 2027 HFC cliff — litigation is the kill vector). Confidence **high**.

## Cross-cutting corrections to the seed / data model
1. **Species tag is too coarse.** All three are HAs, but the deals ride on **affiliated PFCs
   (Ch. 303)** → correct kill-regime is **HB 2071**, and correct tier is effectively **Tier 2**,
   not the generic Tier-3 HA bucket. Add an `affiliated_pfc` field.
2. **`traveling` flag mis-tests municipal HAs.** The seed uses `sponsor_home_county ==
   property_county`. For a *municipal* HA the correct test is **property within city + 5 mi
   (§392.014)**. Rosenberg's Sugar Land/Stafford deals are same-county but **traveling**. Re-run
   the flag against area-of-operation geometry, not county equality, for all Ch. 392 sponsors.
3. **No 2027 cliff** for any of these (HB 21 is HFC-only). The live kill vectors are
   §303.021(d) invalidity for **post-2023 out-of-area PFC acquisitions** (Rosenberg, San Benito)
   and **local litigation** (Plano).
