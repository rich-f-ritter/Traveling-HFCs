# Pleasanton Housing Finance Corporation (PHFC) — Sponsor Profile

**Slug:** `pleasanton-hfc` · **Tier:** 1 (HFC, Ch. 394) · **Seed count:** 58 ·
**Best independent count:** ~68 · **Profile date:** 2026-07-11 ·
**Classification verdict:** CONFIRMS seed Tier 1 — high confidence.

PHFC is the archetype "traveling HFC": a corporation sponsored by a ~11,000-person town
(Pleasanton, Atascosa County) that took multifamily properties off the tax rolls in metros
hundreds of miles from home. It is the named defendant/appellant in the leading traveling-HFC
litigation and the subject of the House Urban Affairs AG-opinion request. It sits squarely
inside HB 21's 1/1/2027 kill regime, and — a key new finding this pass — **it abandoned
(did not brief) its appeals of the temporary injunctions against its model, so those
injunctions now stand.**

---

## 1. Legal species — HFC, Local Gov't Code Chapter 394 (CONFIRMED, primary)

Confirmed as a **housing finance corporation under Local Government Code Chapter 394**, not a
PFC (Ch. 303) or housing authority (Ch. 392), from multiple primary sources:

- **AG opinion request RQ-0566-KP** (Tex. Att'y Gen., 10/2/2024) analyzes PHFC expressly under
  "Texas Local Government Code, Chapter 394" and §394.903(a) — it is the corporation whose
  Ch. 394 boundaries question prompted the request. [RQ0566KP.pdf]
- **Appellate captions** style it "Pleasanton Housing Finance Corporation, a Texas Nonprofit
  Corporation" and litigate the Chapter 394 exemption (2nd COA 02-25-00474-CV; 14th COA
  14-25-00695-CV).
- **TDHCA's "Local Housing Finance Corporation (HFC) Contact List"** carries PHFC as an HFC.

The market/brokers call these "PFC deals"; the entity name and statute chapter control (see
kit trap #1). The exemption theory is **§394.905** (HFC property "exempt … from … all other
taxes" → 100% ad valorem). This is a Ch. 394 / HB 21 entity — the 2027-cliff regime.

## 2. Sponsoring government, home jurisdiction, home county (CONFIRMED)

- **Sponsor:** City of Pleasanton, Texas (created the corporation by council action).
- **Home jurisdiction:** City of Pleasanton (pop. ~11,000).
- **Home county:** **Atascosa County** (south of San Antonio).
- Authorized area under Ch. 394 = Pleasanton's boundaries. **None** of the 58 seed properties
  (nor the ~68 reported) sit in Atascosa County → the entire portfolio is out-of-jurisdiction.

## 5. Kill regime & kill dates — HB 21 Ch. 394 (the 2027 cliff), plus earlier deaths

**Regime: HB 21 (89R, eff. 5/28/2025), Ch. 394.** Because every property is HFC-owned and
out-of-jurisdiction:

- **§13(i) — the 1/1/2027 cliff (primary kill).** A development PHFC owned on **9/1/2025**,
  located outside Pleasanton's boundaries, is **ineligible for the §394.905 exemption after
  1/1/2027** unless *every* host jurisdiction adopted §394.031(d) approving resolutions before
  that date. **No host resolutions are known** for any PHFC property — the opposite is true:
  host cities/counties are suing to block the deals (§7). → **Base-case kill date 1/1/2027**
  (first full-tax bill ~Oct 2027; cash due ~Jan 2028) for the standing out-of-jurisdiction
  portfolio.
- **§13(e) — early death on capital events.** Pre-5/28/2025 deals lose the grandfather at the
  earlier of the 10th tax year or the end of the first tax year after a refi / title conveyance /
  majority-interest transfer. The seed's many 2024–2025 recaps are exactly such events → a
  subset dies **ahead of** 2027 on its own transaction date.
- **§394.031(c)–(d) — outright bar on new travel.** Acquisitions on/after 5/28/2025 outside
  Pleasanton are barred absent host resolutions — reinforced by the injunctions below.
- **§394.9027 audit-forfeiture (independent, possibly earliest).** First annual TDHCA audits
  were due **6/1/2026**; failure to file forfeits the exemption for that tax year. Whether PHFC
  filed for any property is an **open question** — TDHCA's monitoring lists still read "TBD" on
  7/11/2026. Non-filing would kill 2026, before the 2027 cliff.
- **Injunctions already suppress exemptions NOW** in Lake Worth, Arlington, Fort Worth, and
  Missouri City, and (via TRO) in the Hays CAD for San Marcos/Kyle — pre-2027 kills by court
  order (§7).

**Host resolutions adopted:** none found. Track city-council agendas as removals if any appear.

## 7. Litigation / regulatory / AG / news exposure — current status

**A pattern emerges: PHFC has retreated from defending the traveling model on appeal.**

- **Lake Worth — 2nd COA No. 02-25-00474-CV (was 15th COA 15-25-00110-CV).** TI granted
  **6/3/2025** (348th Dist. Ct., Tarrant, No. 348-364430-25) barring PHFC from buying property or
  obtaining exemptions in Lake Worth and barring Tarrant Appraisal District from granting them.
  After a jurisdictional fight, the case moved from the new 15th COA to the Fort Worth 2nd COA.
  **On 1/15/2026 the 2nd COA dismissed PHFC's appeal for want of prosecution — PHFC filed no
  brief.** → **The Lake Worth injunction stands.** *(This reconciles the seed: "02-25-00474-CV"
  is not a separate appraisal case — it is the transferred Lake Worth injunction appeal; its
  appraisal-district dimension is the enjoined TAD exemption.)*
- **Arlington & Fort Worth — 2nd COA No. 02-25-00475-CV (was 15th COA 15-25-00111-CV).** TIs
  granted 6/3/2025 (No. 348-363561-25) for Arlington and Fort Worth; appellants were Pecos,
  Pleasanton, La Villa, and Maverick County HFCs jointly. **On 1/15/2026 the 2nd COA dismissed
  Pleasanton's, La Villa's, and Maverick's appeals for want of prosecution (no briefs); only
  Pecos's appeal survives** (restyled *Pecos HFC v. City of Arlington and City of Fort Worth*).
  A companion, 02-25-00476-CV, is *Pecos HFC v. City of Haltom City*. → **Injunctions against
  PHFC stand.**
- **Missouri City — 14th COA No. 14-25-00695-CV.** TI granted **6/9/2025** (240th Dist. Ct.,
  Fort Bend, No. 25-DCV-328899) for the City of Missouri City and Sienna Parks & Levee
  Improvement District. **PHFC filed an unopposed motion to dismiss its own appeal (10/20/2025);
  the 14th COA dismissed it 10/28/2025 with mandate issued immediately.** → PHFC abandoned;
  injunction stands.
- **Hays County / City of San Marcos v. Pleasanton HFC & Pecos HFC** (Case No. 25-1185-DCB).
  TRO sought to stop the Hays CAD from granting exemptions on San Marcos & Kyle properties.
  County Judge Becerra: proposed exemptions would pull **>$227M** off local rolls, ~$500K/yr
  county loss. A 5/5/2025 hearing was not held; action may be refiled. (KXAN/Yahoo confirm a TRO
  against both HFCs.)
- **AG opinion request RQ-0566-KP** (House Urban Affairs, 10/2/2024): asks whether Ch. 394
  (§394.903(a), "must be located within the local government") lets an HFC own residential
  development outside its sponsor's boundaries — framed on PHFC's Weatherford (Parker Co.) deal.
  **Status: no formal opinion located as of 7/11/2026 — treat as pending/unissued.** A separate
  expedited request by **Sen. Bettencourt (RQ-0596-KP, 4/25/2025)** was **declined by AG Paxton
  on 6/3/2025, citing the pending litigation** (per law-firm coverage). Net: the retroactive-
  reach question (pre-HB 21 §394.903(a); back-assessment under Tax Code §11.43(i)) remains open.
- **News:** Pleasanton Express (the definitive local coverage — 68-property portfolio, fees,
  board), Fort Worth Report, Texas Housers, Sen. Bettencourt's press, News4SA. Related
  same-pattern suit: **Williamson County v. Cameron County HFC** (TRO obtained ~3/2025) —
  not PHFC, but corroborates the statewide crackdown and names Pleasanton as an exemplar.
