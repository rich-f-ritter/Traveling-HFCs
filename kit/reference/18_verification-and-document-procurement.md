## B. Texas-law verification

1. **Back-assessment: 5 years — confirmed, with the right cite.** Erroneously *allowed
   exemptions*: Tax Code **§11.43(i)** — five preceding years, added to the roll via §25.21
   mechanics. (Note §25.21 itself, for *omitted* property, was cut to **3 years real / 2 years
   personal** by HB 1090 in 2021 — don't cite §25.21 alone for the 5-year reach.)
2. **Tax calendar — confirmed.** Lien attaches Jan 1 (§32.01); bills by ~Oct 1 (§31.01);
   delinquent Feb 1 following (§31.02).
3. **Non-disclosure state — confirmed** (no mandatory sales-price disclosure; §552.149/§22.27
   keep obtained sales data confidential).
4. **HFC subject to the Public Information Act — confirmed, now express:** HB 21 added Loc.
   Gov't Code **§394.0045** — Ch. 552 applies to **all records** of an HFC; Ch. 551 (open
   meetings) to its proceedings.
5. **TDHCA audit publication — statute confirmed** (§394.9027(c): findings summary published
   within 60 days). As of 7/11/2026 TDHCA's HFC page lists developments/reports as **"TBD"** —
   the Sarah's filing status is not publicly determinable yet; the 60-day window on 6/1/2026
   filings runs to ~end of July 2026.
