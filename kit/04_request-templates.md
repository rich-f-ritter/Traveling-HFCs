# 04 — Request Templates (TPIA)

Mechanics that apply to all of these (verified): submit via the body's **designated**
channel — posted portal or designated email — per Gov't Code §552.234 (a body that posts
designated addresses need not answer requests sent elsewhere). Production must be
**"prompt"** (§552.221); if the body can't produce within 10 business days it must certify
that in writing with a date certain — calendar a day-10 follow-up. Ask for electronic
copies to avoid per-page charges. If the body seeks an AG ruling to withhold, that's a
~45-business-day detour — narrow, specific requests draw fewer ruling requests.

---

## Template A — To an HFC / its sponsor city (per-deal document set)

> **Public Information Act Request — [HFC NAME] / [PROPERTY NAME]**
>
> Pursuant to the Texas Public Information Act, Gov't Code ch. 552, and Tex. Loc. Gov't
> Code **§394.0045** (which applies chapter 552 to all records of a housing finance
> corporation), I request the following records of [HFC NAME] and/or the City/County of
> [SPONSOR] relating to the multifamily property known as [PROPERTY NAME], [ADDRESS],
> [COUNTY] County, Texas, and related entities [SPE/LLC NAMES]:
>
> 1. The ground lease (with all amendments and exhibits) between [HFC] and [TENANT LLC];
> 2. The memorandum of understanding or similar agreement with the developer, and all
>    amendments;
> 3. The company/operating agreement of any [HFC]-affiliated managing-member entity for
>    the project;
> 4. Any audit, compliance, or fee agreement relating to the project;
> 5. All board agendas, minutes, and resolutions referencing the project or its developer
>    entities, from [DATE RANGE];
> 6. The annual compliance audit(s) submitted or prepared under Loc. Gov't Code §394.9027
>    for the project, and any underwriting assessment prepared under §394.905(b);
> 7. All schedules or lists of multifamily properties in which [HFC] holds or has held an
>    ownership interest, from January 1, 2020 to present.
>
> I request electronic copies. If any portion is withheld, please cite the specific
> exception and produce all segregable portions. If the records cannot be produced within
> ten business days, please provide the written certification required by §552.221(d).

*Item 7 is the portfolio-census question — one request per sponsor yields their whole list.*

## Template B — To TDHCA (the statewide census)

> **Public Information Act Request — HFC/PFC compliance filings**
>
> Pursuant to Gov't Code ch. 552, I request: (1) all compliance audit reports received by
> the Department under Loc. Gov't Code §394.9027 (housing finance corporations) and
> §303.0426 (public facility corporations) from January 1, 2026 to the date of this
> request; (2) the Department's current list or register of multifamily developments
> subject to HFC or PFC compliance monitoring, including development name, address,
> county, and sponsoring corporation; (3) all requests for extension of the June 1, 2026
> audit deadline and the Department's dispositions; and (4) any summary reports prepared
> under §394.9027(c). Per §394.9027(k) I understand tenant-identifying information may be
> redacted; I request all remaining portions electronically.

## Template C — To an appraisal district (per-property exemption file)

> Pursuant to Gov't Code ch. 552, I request, for account [ACCOUNT NO.] ([ADDRESS]): the
> application(s) for exemption filed under Tax Code §11.11 or Loc. Gov't Code §394.905 /
> ch. 303, all correspondence regarding the grant, denial, or review of such exemption,
> and any notices issued under Tax Code §11.43(i). I acknowledge portions of exemption
> applications may be confidential under Tax Code §11.48 and request all segregable
> non-confidential portions electronically.

*§11.48 means expect redactions or an AG-ruling detour on parts of Template C — the grant
date and correspondence usually survive, which is what the census needs.*

## Follow-up (day 10, if silent)

> On [DATE] I submitted the attached request via [PORTAL]. More than ten business days
> have elapsed without production or the written certification required by Gov't Code
> §552.221(d). Please treat this as a request for immediate compliance; absent a response
> I will refer the matter to the Attorney General's Open Records Division.
