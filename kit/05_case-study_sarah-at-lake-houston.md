## The economics (why these unwinds are investable)

- Exemption worth ≈ **$1.38M/yr** (full accrual $1.53M at a ~$65.7M assessment, 2.331628%,
  9 jurisdictions); HFC took 10% of savings + $300K closing fee + $100/unit compliance.
- Rents: the **market cohort held** (~$1,470–1,500 effective, mix-weighted) while the
  restricted cohort sat −8.5% median below its own market marks → **+$549K/yr recapture**
  at unwind (stabilized), concentrated in ~96 truly-below-market units.
- The two-clocks problem: tax snaps back 100% on the first post-death lien date; rent
  recapture ramps over 12–24 months of lease roll. The transition-year gap is a buyer cost.
- Capital stack: $58.3M senior ($166.6K/unit) > ~$54M value ($154K/unit) → senior impaired,
  pref (29th St, 11% PIK) and ~$15M common wiped; special servicer runs the sale. **The
  exemption structure did not cause the distress (a 2022 floating-rate buy did), but its
  statutory death sets the deadline.**

## Structural terms that generalize (look for these in every deal)

- **$10 voluntary reconveyance** any time on written request (MOU §H) + **$1.00 mandatory
  fee transfer if the exemption is lost in the first 20 years other than by developer
  default (MOU §A.1)** — the statutory kill arguably self-executes the $1 transfer.
  PHFC's Weatherford template in RQ-0566-KP shows the same $1 clause — it's boilerplate.
- HFC-ward **Purchase Option** priced to never be exercised (FMV assuming exemption + 125%
  of capital + HFC pays costs + 4% fee to sponsor); **30-day ROFR** (+90 days to close),
  12-month sale window, >10% price cut re-triggers, sale extinguishes both.
- Regulatory Agreement dies with HFC ownership; **sole remedy specific performance**; no
  clawback; vacant units keep low-income character; 60-day cure.
- **Exclusive venue in the sponsor's home county** (Atascosa) — assume the same trap in
  every PHFC deal; solve with deed-in-escrow, never litigation.
- Watch for covenant/roll mismatches (Sarah: 175 required, 162 coded, form certificate
  says 160) — back-assessment exposure under Tax Code §11.43(i).
