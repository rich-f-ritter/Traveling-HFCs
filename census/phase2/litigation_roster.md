# Phase 2 — Litigation Roster (named properties + values from active suits)

*Harvested 2026-07-11 from public-record / free sources (court records via midpage/justia, county
press releases, local news). Companion to `litigation_roster.json`. Constraint honored: no gated
appraisal-portal values; every property carries a source URL. Values marked "reported" are from
news/court coverage, not scraped from a paywalled roll.*

for suits where reporting cites a jurisdiction/value but does not itemize the parcel. Plus 3

## 1. Tarrant County ARB back-assessment drive — Pecos & peers ($974M / 28 sites)

- **Filed:** 5/28/2025 — Tarrant County filed **28 petitions** with the TAD Appraisal Review Board
  challenging the "public property" exclusion on **28 sites**, total **2025 appraised value
  $974,000,000**, seeking removal for the current year **plus the prior 5 years** (Tax Code
  §11.43(i)/§25.21). TAD chief appraiser **Joe Don Bobbitt** named as a defendant.
- **THE 28-SITE LIST IS NOT PUBLICLY ITEMIZED.** Neither the Tarrant County news release nor the
  KERA/Fort Worth Report coverage lists the individual sites, addresses, or per-site values. This
  is the single biggest remaining gap — the itemized list would require the ARB petition documents
  themselves (public record via TAD/ARB or Tarrant County District Clerk, not yet pulled) or a
  TAD roll sweep for exempt owners `%PECOS HOUSING FINANCE%` etc.
- **Named Tarrant property confirmed inside this footprint:**
  - **Jefferson North Collins / "Zenith" Apartments**, Arlington — Pecos HFC — TAD account
    **42642581** (Site 800053142, APTExempt) — **~$1.7M/yr** combined lost taxes (reported).
- Sources: [Tarrant County release](https://www.tarrantcountytx.gov/en/news/2025/tarrant-county-files-petitions-with-the-tad-appraisal-review-boa.html) ·
  [KERA 6/23/2025](https://www.keranews.org/news/2025-06-23/tarrant-county-plans-to-crack-down-on-housing-nonprofits-that-claimed-tax-exempt-status) ·
  [TAD account 42642581](https://www.tad.org/property?account=42642581)

## 5. Hays County / City of San Marcos v. Pecos HFC & Pleasanton HFC (~$227–230M)

- **Cause 25-1185-DCB** (207th Dist. Ct., Hays Co.), filed **4/30/2025**; **TRO** to stop the Hays
  CAD from granting exemptions on **San Marcos and Kyle** properties. County Judge Becerra:
  proposed exemptions would strip **>$227M** from local rolls, ~**$500K/yr** county loss;
  Hays-wide figure reported as **~$230M**.
- **Individual San Marcos/Kyle parcels NOT itemized** in public reporting → two placeholder records
  (Pecos-San Marcos TX-LIT-0008, Pleasanton-Kyle TX-LIT-0009). Seed context: Pecos has 1 Hays row
  (recap 03/25; partners Nome Capital, Pensam); Pleasanton has 4 Hays rows (Kyle/San Marcos).
- Related named Pecos deal from same coverage: **Kendrick Apartments**, 7324 Skillman St, NE Dallas
  (Dallas Co.) — Pecos HFC + S2 Capital sale-leaseback (TX-LIT-0004; not a Hays parcel).
- Sources: [The Real Deal 4/30/2025](https://therealdeal.com/texas/austin/2025/04/30/hays-county-sues-housing-finance-corporations/) ·

## 6. City of Missouri City v. Maverick County HFC — The Ranch at Sienna

- **Original Petition + TRO + temporary/permanent injunction filed 1/24/2025** (Fort Bend Dist.
  Ct.; task cites 268th — court number to confirm). Ultra vires claim under Ch. 394.
- **NAMED PROPERTY:** **The Ranch at Sienna**, **8811 Sienna Springs Blvd, Missouri City 77459**
  (within Missouri City limits) — Maverick County HFC acquiring. (TX-LIT-0005.)
- **Data correction vs task brief:** the task grouped "Ranch/Ravella at Sienna" under Maverick
  County HFC. In fact these are **two different deals/entities**:
  - **The Ranch at Sienna** (8811 Sienna Springs Blvd) = **Maverick County HFC** (Jan 2025 suit). ✅
  - **Ravella at Sienna** (5330 Sienna Pkwy, outside city limits) = **Fort Bend County HFC**, a
    July 2024 Missouri City suit — the HFC **dropped** that acquisition after the suit (TX-LIT-0006).
    Fort Bend County HFC is a *new sponsor* not in the 335 seed.
- Sources: [Eagle Pass Business Journal 3/2025](https://www.epbusinessjournal.com/2025/03/maverick-county-housing-finance-corporation-sued-by-city-of-missouri-city-texas/) ·
  [Houston Public Media 7/15/2024 (Ravella/Fort Bend Co. HFC)](https://www.houstonpublicmedia.org/articles/housing/2024/07/15/492742/fort-bend-housing-corporation-drops-affordable-housing-project-after-missouri-city-files-suit/)
